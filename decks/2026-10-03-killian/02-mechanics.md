# Stage 2 — Mechanics

- **Pick:** Killian, Ink Duelist
- **Format:** commander
- **Bracket / role target:** Bracket 3. Three Game Changers: Demonic Tutor, Enlightened Tutor, Smothering Tithe. Necropotence is out.
- **Idea reminder:** Imported list. The plan below is what the cards are actually doing.

## Plan on the list — discounted auras

- **How it wins:** Cast Killian, then pile auras on him, **Kor Spiritdancer**, or another body. **Ethereal Armor**, **All That Glitters**, **Tempest Technique**, and double strike (`Battle Mastery`, `Twinblade Blessing`) are the damage. **Unquestioned Authority** makes the body hard to block. **Celestial Mantle** doubles life; it does not end the game.
- **A normal turn:** Land, a cantrip aura for `{0}` or `{1}` because of Killian, draw from **Sram** / **Mesa Enchantress** / **Kor Spiritdancer** / the aura itself. Repeat until the suited creature attacks.
- **Support already in the deck:** enchantress creatures, cantrip auras, protection auras, **Enlightened Tutor**, **Demonic Tutor**.
- **Risk:** One removal spell undoes the turn unless the aura is **Kaya's Ghostform** or a protection spell is in hand. There is no totem armor and no **Lightning Greaves** / **Swiftfoot Boots**. Killian has menace and no haste.

## Second plan fighting the first — graveyard enchantments

- **How it wins:** Mill or surveil (`Ripples of Undeath`, **Doom Whisperer**, **Takenuma**), then **Mantle of the Ancients** puts auras back onto one creature. **Animate Dead** and **Necromancy** reanimate creatures. **Open the Vaults** returns artifacts and enchantments from every graveyard; auras with nothing to enchant do not come back attached.
- **Risk:** **Necropotence** exiles cards you discard, so it does not stock the graveyard this package wants. **Open the Vaults** also returns opponents’ permanents.

## What is not a plan

- **Grim Guardian** drains 1 life per enchantment. That is a clock of many turns.
- **Abdel Adrian** blinks your own board into Soldiers and strips auras.
- **Tinybones Joins Up** mills one card when a legendary enters.
- **The Meathook Massacre** is a real sweeper and a death trigger. The deck has almost nothing that wants creatures to die except **Necrosynthesis**.

## Applied

`Eldrazi Conscription` is in the 100. `Open the Vaults` is out. `Retether` returns your auras onto creatures. `Sigarda's Aid`, `Light-Paws`, `Hyena Umbra`, `Gift of Immortality`, `Swiftfoot Boots`, and `Swords to Plowshares` are in. The sideboard is empty.
