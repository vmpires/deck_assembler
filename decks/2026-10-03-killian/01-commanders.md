### Killian, Ink Duelist

`{W}{B}` Human Warlock. Lifelink, menace. Spells you cast that target a creature cost `{2}` less.

He is a cheap aura host. Auras with “enchant creature” target, so one- and two-mana auras often cost nothing or `{1}` once he is out. Instants that target a creature (`Apostle's Blessing`) get the same discount. Enchantments that do not target (`Necropotence`, `Smothering Tithe`, `Land Tax`) do not.
