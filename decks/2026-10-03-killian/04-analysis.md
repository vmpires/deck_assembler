## What the deck is

Killian makes auras cheap, and the 99 is mostly auras. **32** cards are auras. Enchantress creatures turn those casts into cards. A second, smaller pile mills and tries to get enchantments back from the graveyard. Those two plans do not help each other, because **Necropotence** exiles discarded cards instead of putting them in the graveyard.

The deck draws. It does not reliably kill. Most auras replace themselves and add a small keyword. The auras that turn a creature into a real attacker are a short list. The aura that ends the game by itself, **Eldrazi Conscription**, is in the sideboard.

## Keep — these cards are the deck

**Discount and suit-up:** Killian, `Ethereal Armor`, `All That Glitters`, `Battle Mastery`, `Twinblade Blessing`, `Unquestioned Authority`, `Sage's Reverie`, `Songbirds' Blessing`, `Mantle of the Ancients`, `Tempest Technique`.

**Enchantress:** `Sram, Senior Edificer`, `Mesa Enchantress`, `Kor Spiritdancer`, `Pearl-Ear, Imperial Advisor`, `Hateful Eidolon`. Kor is also a backup voltron body: she gets +2/+2 per aura attached to her.

**Protection that is already here:** `Kaya's Ghostform`, `Shardmage's Rescue`, `Apostle's Blessing`, `Benevolent Blessing`, `Sejiri Shelter`, `Malakir Rebirth`, `Alseid of Life's Bounty`. `Sheltered by Ghosts` and `Chains of Custody` add ward and remove a permanent.

**Mana that matches the plan:** `Land Tax`, `Smothering Tithe`, `Gleaming Splendor`, `Sol Ring`, `Lotus Petal`. Gleaming Splendor makes a Treasure when an opponent draws a second card, and `{2}{W}` can force two players to draw. That feeds both Gleaming and Tithe.

**Tutors:** `Enlightened Tutor` finds an aura or an enchantress piece. `Demonic Tutor` finds anything. Together with Necropotence and Tithe they are why this is Bracket 4.

## Cantrip auras — cards, not damage

These nine draw a card and do very little else: `Angelic Gift` (flying), `Feather of Flight` (flying), `Chosen by Heliod` (+0/+2), `Scourgemark` (+1/+0), `Grisly Transformation` (intimidate), `Pentarch Ward` (protection), `Rune of Mortality` (deathtouch), `Rune of Sustenance` (lifelink), and the flying/intimidate ones already named.

They are correct next to Sram, Mesa, and Kor. They are not a clock. A Killian with four of them is a 2/2 that drew four cards.

## Removal auras — fine, and they are not a win

`Darksteel Mutation`, `Reprobation`, `Heliod's Punishment`, `Minimus Containment`, `Twisted Embrace`, `Chains of Custody`, `Sheltered by Ghosts`, `Coercive Impetus`.

Killian makes these cheap, so they are on plan. `Coercive Impetus` goads and draws. None of them suit up your creature except Sheltered by Ghosts (+1/+0, lifelink, ward).

## Graveyard package — working against Necropotence

| Card | What it actually does here |
| --- | --- |
| `Necropotence` | Best draw spell in the deck. Exiles cards you discard. Skips your draw step. |
| `Ripples of Undeath` | Mills three, then you may pay 1 and 3 life for one of them. This does fill the graveyard. |
| `Doom Whisperer` | 6/6 flying trampler that surveils. A body for auras, and a way to bin them. Five mana, no haste. |
| `Animate Dead` / `Necromancy` | Reanimate a creature. They do not put auras on Killian. |
| `Mantle of the Ancients` | The one recursion spell that attaches auras to your creature. Keep. |
| `Sevinne's Reclamation` | Brings back a permanent with mana value 3 or less. Fine. Flashback is slow. |
| `Open the Vaults` | Returns everyone’s artifacts and enchantments. Auras do not come back attached. Opponents get their permanents too. |
| `Takenuma, Abandoned Mire` | Channel mills three and returns a creature or planeswalker to hand. Narrow. |

**Necrosynthesis** wants other creatures to die, then draws when the enchanted creature dies. This deck does not make things die on purpose. **Leonin Relic-Warder** exiles an artifact or enchantment and gives it back when the cat leaves. That is a blink for an opposing permanent, or a way to fizzle your own, not an engine.

## Cards that do not belong on this plan

- **Abdel Adrian, Gorion's Ward.** Enters and exiles your other nonland permanents until he leaves, one Soldier each. Auras come back unattached. He turns the enchantress board off to make 1/1s with no anthem.
- **Celestial Mantle.** +3/+3 and double your life when the creature hits. Forty life doubled is not a kill. There is no payoff for a huge life total.
- **Grim Guardian.** Each enchantment drains the table for 1. With 32 auras this is a slow bleed, and only if they resolve and stay.
- **Tinybones Joins Up.** One discard on entry. Later, a legendary creature entering mills one and drains 1. Killian is the legendary you cast.
- **Moment of Reckoning.** Seven mana to destroy up to four permanents and/or reanimate. Killian does not reduce it unless the modes target a creature on the stack in a way the spell itself targets — the spell targets whatever you choose, so a creature mode does get the discount. It is still a clunky sweeper next to **The Meathook Massacre**, which is the real one.
- **Urza's Saga.** Chapter III finds a `{0}` or `{1}` artifact: `Sol Ring`, `Lotus Petal`, or `Esper Sentinel`. That is the whole artifact suite. `All That Glitters` likes the Saga and the Construct, and then the well is dry.
- **Michiko's Reign of Truth.** Temporary pump, then a creature that scales with artifacts and enchantments. Acceptable backup attacker. Not an enchantress.

## Mana

31 dedicated lands is reasonable. The five MDFCs (`Fell the Profane`, `Glasswing Grace`, `Malakir Rebirth`, `Razorgrass Ambush`, `Sejiri Shelter`) are spells you can play as lands, so the deck is not flooding as hard as “36 lands” sounds.

Pain and rainbow lands are doing work: `City of Brass`, `Mana Confluence`, `Caves of Koilos`, `Silent Clearing`, `Horizon of Progress`, `Exotic Orchard`, `Reflecting Pool`, `Command Tower`. Fetches into 8 basics: `Marsh Flats`, `Prismatic Vista`, `Fabled Passage`. `Land Tax` wants those basics.

Always-tapped or usually-tapped: `Shadowy Backstreet`, `Valgavoth's Lair`, `Turbulent Moor` (untapped only if opponents control eight lands). `Isolated Chapel` and `Shattered Sanctum` are fine once a land is down. For a two-drop commander, the tapped lands are the part that makes the deck feel slow.

## Holes

1. **No haste.** A suited Killian sits for a turn. `Swiftfoot Boots` gives haste and hexproof, and opponents are the only ones who cannot target the creature, so you can still cast auras onto it. `Lightning Greaves` gives haste and shroud. Shroud stops your own auras, so Greaves only works if the auras are already attached before it is equipped.
2. **No umbra armor.** One destroy effect removes the creature and every aura on it. `Hyena Umbra` (totem armor, +1/+1, first strike, `{W}`) and `Gift of Immortality` are legal and on plan. `Kaya's Ghostform` is the only “come back” aura in the 100.
3. **No Light-Paws.** `Light-Paws, Emperor's Voice` searches an aura onto herself whenever you cast one. She is the Orzhov aura engine this list does not have. Confirmed legal in Commander.
4. **No flash for auras.** `Sigarda's Aid` lets you cast auras as though they had flash. Killian’s discount still applies. Without it, protection auras have to be played on your turn, before the removal.
5. **The kill aura is in the sideboard.** `Eldrazi Conscription` gives +10/+10, trample, and annihilator 2. It is the card this voltron pile is missing. `Dark Ritual` and `Brilliant Restoration` are the other two sideboard cards. Restoration has the same “auras do not reattach” problem as `Open the Vaults`.
6. **Four Game Changers.** Bracket 3 allows three. To sit at high Bracket 3, one of `Demonic Tutor`, `Enlightened Tutor`, `Necropotence`, or `Smothering Tithe` has to come out. The tutors are the most redundant with each other. Necropotence and Tithe are the most powerful engines.

## Where it stands

This is an Orzhov enchantress deck with a voltron commander. The draw is real. The kill is a handful of pump auras and a double-strike aura, and the suited creature dies to the first removal spell. The graveyard cards and Abdel Adrian are a different deck sitting in the same 99. The sideboard already has the aura that ends the game.
