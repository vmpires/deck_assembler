## Game plan

Killian makes spells that target a creature cost {2} less. Auras land cheap, and Sram, Mesa Enchantress, Kor Spiritdancer, Pearl-Ear, and Light-Paws draw or tutor off them. Sigarda's Aid lets those auras be cast at instant speed. The suited creature attacks with haste from Swiftfoot Boots. Hyena Umbra, Gift of Immortality, and Kaya's Ghostform keep it from staying dead. Eldrazi Conscription is the one-shot kill. Retether and Mantle of the Ancients put auras from the graveyard back onto a creature.
