## Game plan

Lockdown Auras go on their best creatures. Pacifism, Darksteel Mutation, Reprobation, Cooped Up, Petrify, Planar Disruption, and Realmbreaker's Grasp stop those creatures from attacking. Dog Umbra does that while an opponent controls the creature, and it is umbra armor on yours. Ossification, Sheltered by Ghosts, Chains of Custody, and Faith's Fetters exile or shut off a problem and still count as Auras. At your end step, each opponent loses life equal to the Aura count, and you gain that much life once.

The other wins are there because the drain is slow. Grim Guardian drains 1 more from each opponent whenever an enchantment enters. Sanguine Bond makes one opponent lose life equal to the life you gained. It is not paired with Exquisite Blood. Sigil of the Empty Throne, Archon of Sun's Grace, and Hallowed Haunting make flying attackers when you cast enchantments. Nykthos Paragon, once each turn, puts +1/+1 counters on your team equal to the life Eriette just gained. Ethereal Armor, All That Glitters, Spirit Mantle, and Battle Mastery can go on Eriette, Light-Paws, or Kor Spiritdancer. Casting a lockdown Aura still triggers Light-Paws, so she tutors a second Aura onto herself. Bolas's Citadel can also tap and sacrifice ten nonland permanents to make each opponent lose 10 life. Doomwake Giant shrinks their board whenever an enchantment enters. Mantle of the Ancients and Retether put Auras from the graveyard back onto creatures.

Mana is the other add. Killian makes Auras that target a creature cost {2} less. Pearl-Ear, Danitha Capashen, and Starfield Mystic reduce enchantments. Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Orzhov Signet, Talisman of Hierarchy, Fellwar Stone, and Smothering Tithe make mana. Land Tax and Knight of the White Orchid find lands when you are behind. Nyx Lotus and Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx add mana from devotion. Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth plus Cabal Coffers is the black burst. Bolas's Citadel lets you cast the top card by paying life instead of mana, which the life gain supports.

Greater Auramancy gives your other enchantments shroud, so the lockdown Auras are harder to target, and it gives shroud to enchanted creatures you control. Wraths still clear the board.
