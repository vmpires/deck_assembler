# Stage 2 — Mechanics

- **Pick:** Eriette of the Charmed Apple
- **Format:** commander
- **Bracket / role target:** high Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4
- **Idea reminder:** Orzhov Auras. The end step drains each opponent for the Aura count and you gain that much life once. Enchanted creatures cannot attack you. The deck still needs a way to attack. Orzhov has no green ramp, so rocks and cheap Auras do that job. Killian, Ink Duelist can sit in the 99: Auras that target a creature cost {2} less. That is an engine, not a second copy of his deck.

Left out of every plan: Ghostly Prison, Sphere of Safety, and the Sanguine Bond plus Exquisite Blood loop. Exquisite Blood is about $41. The loop is an opt-in combo, not the default.

## Plans

### A. Suit your own creatures

- **How it wins:** Ethereal Armor and All That Glitters turn one of your creatures into the killer. Eriette's end step drains everyone else for the Aura count. Four Auras and three opponents means each of them loses 4 and you gain 4. Dog Umbra, Pacifism, Darksteel Mutation, or Reprobation goes on the one creature that would kill you. Sheltered by Ghosts exiles a problem, pumps your creature, and still counts as an Aura.
- **A normal turn:** A cheap creature, often Killian or Light-Paws, then an Aura on it. Killian makes that Aura cost {2} less. Attack. The end step drains. Pearl-Ear makes later Auras cheaper and draws once the creature is already modified.
- **Support categories:** pump Auras, one or two lockdown Auras, enchantress draw, a second host, rocks
- **Exemplars:** Killian, Ink Duelist, Pearl-Ear, Imperial Advisor, Light-Paws, Emperor's Voice, Ethereal Armor, All That Glitters, Dog Umbra
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3. Combat is the fast clock. The drain cleans up the players you are not attacking.
- **Profile fit:** Combat, card draw, and a real kill. Kor Spiritdancer, Sram, and Mesa Enchantress draw off Auras. Sage's Reverie draws for the pile already on creatures. This is the plan that keeps you attacking.

### B. The end step grows a board

- **How it wins:** Casting enchantments makes other attackers and adds to the drain. Sigil of the Empty Throne makes a 4/4 flying Angel. Archon of Sun's Grace makes a 2/2 flying Pegasus. Hallowed Haunting (~$8) makes a Spirit whose size is your Spirit count, and at seven enchantments your creatures fly. Grim Guardian drains 1 from each opponent whenever an enchantment enters. Nykthos Paragon, once each turn, puts +1/+1 counters on each creature you control equal to the life Eriette just gained. Doomwake Giant gives opposing creatures -1/-1 whenever an enchantment enters.
- **A normal turn:** An enchantress, then two cheap Auras. Make a flyer, tick Grim Guardian, attack with the token, and let the end step drain and grow the team.
- **Support categories:** token enchantments, life-gain payoffs, constellation drain, enchantress draw, rocks
- **Exemplars:** Sigil of the Empty Throne, Archon of Sun's Grace, Hallowed Haunting, Grim Guardian, Nykthos Paragon, Doomwake Giant
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3, slower than Plan A. The kill is a board of flyers plus the drain, so one removal spell takes one attacker.
- **Profile fit:** Tokens, incremental value, and combat. Starfield of Nyx (~$15) turns non-Aura enchantments into creatures once you control five, and returns one enchantment from the graveyard on your upkeep. Auras stay Auras. Heliod, Sun-Crowned (~$22) puts a single counter on one creature when you gain life. Nykthos Paragon hits the whole team.

### C. Auras on their creatures

- **How it wins:** Pacifism, Darksteel Mutation, Reprobation, Planar Disruption, and Faith's Fetters sit on their best creatures. Those creatures cannot attack. Dog Umbra stops a creature from attacking or blocking while an opponent controls it, and it is umbra armor if it is on your creature. Eriette drains every turn for the Aura count. Hateful Eidolon draws when an enchanted creature dies, once for each Aura that was on it. Your own creatures are a backup, not the main clock.
- **A normal turn:** Eriette, then a lockdown Aura on the scariest attacker. Cast another Aura. End step, each opponent loses life. Repeat until the Aura count is the win.
- **Support categories:** lockdown Auras, drain, enchantress draw, a few of your own attackers, rocks
- **Exemplars:** Pacifism, Darksteel Mutation, Reprobation, Planar Disruption, Dog Umbra, Hateful Eidolon
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3 on the slow side. The clock does not start until several Auras are down.
- **Profile fit:** This is the pillow list. A board of "can't attack" Auras keeps combat from happening. Ghostly Prison and Sphere of Safety are outside this plan. Skip this one if you want to be the attacker.

## Pick

**C. Auras on their creatures**, with extra win conditions and acceleration added. The list is in `03-deck.md` and `deck.cod`. Ghostly Prison, Sphere of Safety, and Exquisite Blood stayed out.
