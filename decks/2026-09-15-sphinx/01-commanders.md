# Stage 1 — Commanders / shells

- **Idea:** Sphinx deck (typal / Sphinx-in-the-command-zone)
- **Format:** commander
- **Bracket / role target:** Bracket 3 (Upgraded)
- **Colors / identity (if known):** Blue-centered; white and black optional
- **Scryfall queries used:**
  - `is:commander t:sphinx f:commander`
  - `t:sphinx f:commander unique:cards` (82 Sphinxes)
  - `t:sphinx f:commander unique:cards id<=u` (60)
  - `t:sphinx f:commander unique:cards id<=uw` (70)
  - `t:sphinx f:commander unique:cards id<=wub` (80)

Skipped on profile: **Medomai the Ageless** (extra turns), **Sharuum the Hegemon** (graveyard reanimator), **Alhammarret, High Arbiter** (name-lock prison). Tivit / Elenda and Azor / Ormos are Sphinxes but they do not make a Sphinx deck.

## Options

Rank 5–8. For Pauper, these are archetype shells, not commanders.

### 1. Unesh, Criosphinx Sovereign

- **Identity / colors:** U
- **Why it fits the pitch:** The only commander that actually *says Sphinx*. {2} off every Sphinx, and each Sphinx ETB (including Unesh) Fact-or-Fictions you. ~60 identity-legal Sphinxes is enough to be a real tribe.
- **Likely plans:** Typal flyers, ETB value, control finishers (Consecrated Sphinx is a Game Changer and ~$31).
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** Bracket 3
- **Risk:** Slow and mana-hungry; pile-choosing discards half the hits; no white removal.
- **Skip if:** you want Esper interaction more than a Sphinx-matters engine.

### 2. Yennett, Cryptic Sovereign

- **Identity / colors:** WUB
- **Why it fits the pitch:** A Sphinx that attacks for free odd-cost spells. Combat + spellslinger + Esper answers, and you can still run a high Sphinx count (many Sphinxes are odd MV; 41 odd-MV Sphinxes in Esper).
- **Likely plans:** Odd-cost Sphinx tribal, top-of-deck manipulation, control-combat hybrid.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** Bracket 3 (can lean 4 if you pack extra tutors / Game Changers)
- **Risk:** The 99 wants odd MV, so even-cost staples fight the commander.
- **Skip if:** you want pure typal Unesh, not an odd-cost puzzle.

### 3. Atemsis, All-Seeing

- **Identity / colors:** U
- **Why it fits the pitch:** A Sphinx with a built-in win: connect, reveal six different mana values in hand, they lose. Filter via loot, then fly over.
- **Likely plans:** Control-value, diverse curve, combat alternate win. Sphinxes as the flying team, not a lord.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** Bracket 3
- **Risk:** The win is linear and telegraphed; still a beatdown deck at heart.
- **Skip if:** you want “Sphinx tribal” rather than “Sphinx that wins the game.”

### 4. Isperia the Inscrutable

- **Identity / colors:** UW
- **Why it fits the pitch:** Combat Sphinx that tutors a flyer if you name a card in their hand. Azorius Sphinx/flyers with white removal.
- **Likely plans:** Combat-control, flying typal, name-and-tutor value.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** Bracket 2–3
- **Risk:** The trigger is conditional and politically noisy; weaker than Unesh at actually rewarding Sphinxes.
- **Skip if:** you hate naming cards from hidden information.

### 5. Isperia, Supreme Judge

- **Identity / colors:** UW
- **Why it fits the pitch:** Control Sphinx: you draw whenever someone attacks you. White + blue interaction, Sphinx/flyer board.
- **Likely plans:** Pillow-fort-adjacent control (draw, not stax), flyers as win.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** Bracket 3
- **Risk:** Rewards being attacked, not playing Sphinxes. Can feel like Azorius goodstuff with a Sphinx sticker.
- **Skip if:** you want the 99 to be Sphinx-matters.

### 6. Azor, the Lawbringer

- **Identity / colors:** UW
- **Why it fits the pitch:** Attack to pay {X}{W}{U}{U} for X cards and X life. Big-mana control Sphinx.
- **Likely plans:** Combat-control, ramp into X-draw, Azorius flyers.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** Bracket 3
- **Risk:** ETB shuts off opponents’ instants/sorceries for a turn — prison-adjacent. We would keep that as a timing trick, not a lock.
- **Skip if:** even a one-turn silence feels too staxy.

### 7. Raffine, Scheming Seer

- **Identity / colors:** WUB
- **Why it fits the pitch:** Cheapest Sphinx commander. Attack-to-connive is a strong combat-value engine in Esper.
- **Likely plans:** Aggro-value, +1/+1 counters, discard synergies. **Not Sphinx tribal.**
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** Bracket 3–4
- **Risk:** The deck will not look or play like Sphinxes unless you force it (and Raffine does not care).
- **Skip if:** the pitch is “a Sphinx deck,” which it is.

### 8. Arjun, the Shifting Flame

- **Identity / colors:** UR
- **Why it fits the pitch:** Spellslinger Sphinx: every spell replaces your hand. Fits your spellslinger taste, not the tribe.
- **Likely plans:** Instant-sorcery volume, wheels, Izzet control-combo.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** Bracket 3
- **Risk:** Hand-cycling every spell can feel chaotic (profile hard-no on chaos). Almost no extra Sphinxes in red.
- **Skip if:** you want tribal or a stable hand.

## Stop

Waiting for a pick. Do not write mechanics or a list yet.

**Recommendation:** Unesh if you want the Sphinxes to matter; Yennett if you want Esper and a Sphinx that still asks the 99 to be Sphinx-shaped (odd costs).
