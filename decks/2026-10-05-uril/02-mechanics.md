# Stage 2 — Mechanics

- **Pick:** Uril, the Miststalker
- **Format:** commander
- **Bracket / role target:** high Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4
- **Idea reminder:** Auras, with other creatures that can win when Uril is taxed or destroyed. Interaction and ramp are required in every plan.

Shared shell, all three plans:

- **Ramp.** Land Auras do both jobs. Wild Growth, Utopia Sprawl, Fertile Ground, and Wolfwillow Haven add mana and count as enchantments for Ethereal Armor and Ancestral Mask. Nature's Lore, Three Visits, Farseek, Birds of Paradise, and Sanctum Weaver (taps for mana equal to your enchantment count) cover the rest. Uril is four mana and three colors.
- **Interaction.** Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Generous Gift, and Chaos Warp answer creatures and other permanents. Heroic Intervention (~$18), Boros Charm, and Flawless Maneuver stop a wrath from taking the suited creature and the Auras with it. Winds of Rath destroys every creature that is not enchanted, so your suited attackers stay.
- **Auras that move.** Ethereal Armor, All That Glitters, Ancestral Mask, Daybreak Coronet, Armadillo Cloak, and Spirit Mantle are the pump. They do not say "Uril." Shield of the Oversoul gives the green half (indestructible) and the white half (flying). Uril and any other green-and-white creature get both. A green-only host gets indestructible only.

## Plans

### A. Next host

- **How it wins:** Uril still kills with commander damage when he sticks. When he does not, the same Aura pile goes on a second hexproof or hard-to-block creature. Mantle of the Ancients pulls Auras out of the graveyard onto whichever creature you still control. Retether (~$18) returns every Aura in the graveyard onto creatures.
- **A normal turn:** Ramp, play a one- or two-mana hexproof creature, then cast Uril. Auras go on Uril. If he dies, the next Aura and Mantle go on Silhana Ledgewalker, Sigarda, or Kor Spiritdancer, and that creature attacks.
- **Support categories:** backup hosts, Aura recovery, pump, ramp, spot removal, wrath protection
- **Exemplars:** Kor Spiritdancer (gets +2/+2 per Aura and may draw when you cast one), Silhana Ledgewalker (hexproof, blocked only by flyers), Sigarda, Host of Herons (flying, hexproof, opponents cannot force sacrifices), Light-Paws, Emperor's Voice (a cast Aura tutors a second Aura onto her), Mantle of the Ancients, Thrun, the Last Troll (hexproof, uncounterable, regenerates)
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3. One suited attacker is still the kill. The backup host is what keeps a removal spell from ending the game.
- **Profile fit:** Combat and a real clock. Light-Paws makes games more samey, because she tutors. Retether is recovery, not a graveyard deck. Argothian Enchantress draws on enchantments and has shroud, and she is about $65, so she stays optional.

### B. Enchantments make the army

- **How it wins:** Casting enchantments draws cards and creates other attackers. Sigil of the Empty Throne makes a 4/4 flying Angel. Archon of Sun's Grace makes a 2/2 flying Pegasus. Hallowed Haunting (~$8) makes a Spirit whose size is the number of Spirits you control, and at seven enchantments your creatures fly. Setessan Champion grows a counter and draws whenever an enchantment enters. Uril wears Auras when he is alive. The Angels, Pegasi, and Champion attack when he is not.
- **A normal turn:** Sanctum Weaver or a land Aura, an enchantress, then an Aura or Sigil. Draw, make a flyer, attack with that flyer. Uril comes down as the biggest threat, not the only one.
- **Support categories:** enchantress draw, token attackers, cost reduction, ramp, interaction
- **Exemplars:** Sythis, Harvest's Hand, Enchantress's Presence, Setessan Champion, Sigil of the Empty Throne, Archon of Sun's Grace, Hallowed Haunting
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3, a step slower than Plan A. Card draw is the engine. The kill is a board of flyers plus one suited creature.
- **Profile fit:** Incremental value, tokens, and combat. Jukai Naturalist and Herald of the Pantheon make enchantments cost {1} less. Starfield of Nyx (~$15) turns your non-Aura enchantments into creatures once you control five, and it returns one enchantment from the graveyard on your upkeep. Auras stay Auras.

### C. Several suited attackers

- **How it wins:** Two or three creatures each wear an Aura and attack in the same combat. No single pile has to stick. Ethereal Armor and Ancestral Mask are still huge on whoever wears them, because they count your enchantments. Rancor, Unflinching Courage, Armadillo Cloak, and Spirit Mantle go on different bodies. Winds of Rath clears everyone else's creatures and leaves yours.
- **A normal turn:** Land Aura, a small hexproof creature, a second creature, then one Aura on each. Attack with both. Uril joins when mana allows and takes the strongest Aura.
- **Support categories:** cheap hexproof bodies, one Aura per attacker, board wipe that spares enchanted creatures, ramp, spot removal
- **Exemplars:** Gladecover Scout, Witchstalker, Silhana Ledgewalker, Rancor, Unflinching Courage, Winds of Rath
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3. Wider than Plan A, so one kill spell takes one attacker. Each threat is smaller until the enchantment count is high.
- **Profile fit:** Combat. The least all-in of the three. Kor Spiritdancer still draws. Daybreak Coronet wants a creature that already has an Aura, so she belongs on the creature that already connected, often Uril.
