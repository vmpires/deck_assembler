# Stage 3 — Deck

- **Name:** Uril, the Miststalker
- **Format:** commander
- **Commander / shell:** Uril, the Miststalker
- **Mechanic:** Next host. Uril wears the Auras. Hexproof and hard-to-block creatures wear them when he is taxed or destroyed.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3 (2 Game Changers)
- **Game Changers (EDH):** Enlightened Tutor, Worldly Tutor
- **Expensive cards to flag:** Boseiju, Who Endures ($49.80), Enlightened Tutor ($37.75), Arid Mesa ($30.38), Worldly Tutor ($30.18), Delighted Halfling ($24.80)

## Game plan

Ramp with land Auras and green land tutors, then cast Uril and stack pump, evasion, and umbra armor. Ethereal Armor, Ancestral Mask, and All That Glitters scale with the enchantment count, so the land Auras are part of the kill. If Uril dies, the same Auras go on Gladecover Scout, Silhana Ledgewalker, Witchstalker, Thrun, Kor Spiritdancer, Light-Paws, Setessan Champion, or Sigarda, Host of Herons. Mantle of the Ancients and Retether put Auras from the graveyard onto a creature that is still alive. Light-Paws tutors a second Aura onto herself whenever you cast one. Commander damage from Uril is the fast kill. A suited flyer or an unblockable Ledgewalker closes when he does not.
