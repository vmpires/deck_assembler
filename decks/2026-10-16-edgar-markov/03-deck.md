## Game plan

Cast cheap **Vampire** spells so **Edgar Markov** (usually in the command zone) prints 1/1 tokens. **Shared Animosity**, **Legion Lieutenant**, **Stromkirk Captain**, **Captivating Vampire**, and **Metallic Mimic** turn the swarm sideways. **Sanctum Seeker** and **Bloodletter of Aclazotz** drain while you attack. The midrange spine — **Bloodline Keeper**, **Edgar, Charmed Groom**, **Champion of Dusk**, **Twilight Prophet**, **Olivia Voldaren** — keeps a real board after the first wipe so you are not only 1/1s. Cast Edgar himself when the team is wide enough that his attack trigger ends someone.

**Kindred Dominance** is the one-sided reset. **Rising of the Day** and greaves let tokens and Edgar attack immediately. No aristocrats package, no stax, no extra-turn chains, no two-card infinites.
