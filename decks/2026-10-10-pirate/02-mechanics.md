# Stage 2 — Mechanics

- **Pick:** Admiral Beckett Brass
- **Format:** commander
- **Bracket / role target:** high Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4
- **Idea reminder:** Grixis Pirates. Other Pirates get +1/+1. At your end step, gain control of target nonland permanent controlled by a player who was dealt combat damage by three or more Pirates this turn. The steal stays.

Left out of every plan: Pitiless Plunderer (Treasure on death; that is the infinite piece with a free sacrifice outlet). Timestream Navigator (extra turn). Ragavan, Nimble Pilferer is a one-mana Pirate that makes a Treasure and steals a card, and it is about $41, so it stays optional. Roaming Throne is about $52. None of the exemplars below are Game Changers.

## Plans

### A. Boarding party

- **How it wins:** Lords and a wide attack. Corsair Captain gives other Pirates +1/+1 and makes a Treasure when it enters. Dire Fleet Neckbreaker gives attacking Pirates +2/+0. Shared Animosity adds +1/+0 for each other attacking creature that shares a type, so a Pirate attack scales with the crew. Combat damage is the clock. Brass's end step takes the permanent that would stop the next swing. Port Razer untaps your creatures and adds a combat when it connects, and it cannot attack a player it has already attacked this turn, so the extra combat is a second swing, not a loop.
- **A normal turn:** Cast a Pirate or a lord, attack with the fliers already on the board, and draw if Coastal Piracy sees a connect. If three Pirates hit the same player, take their best nonland permanent at end step.
- **Support categories:** anthems, evasion, combat card draw, a few cheap Pirates, interaction
- **Exemplars:** Corsair Captain, Dire Fleet Neckbreaker, Shared Animosity, Malcolm, Keen-Eyed Navigator, Coastal Piracy, Warkite Marauder, Port Razer
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4. The clock is combat. The steal is how you answer a problem permanent without spending a card.
- **Profile fit:** Combat and tribal. Shared Animosity is about $6. Malcolm makes one Treasure per opponent dealt damage, so the mana shows up when the attack works.

### B. Treasure crew

- **How it wins:** The attack pays for the rest of the turn. Malcolm makes a Treasure for each opponent your Pirates damage. Captain Lannery Storm makes a Treasure when she attacks and gets +1/+0 whenever you sacrifice a Treasure. Corsair Captain makes one on the way in. Gemcutter Buccaneer makes a tapped Treasure whenever it or another Pirate enters, and your Treasures become Equipment that grant +2/+0 and equip a Pirate for {1}. Captain Storm, Cosmium Raider puts a +1/+1 counter on a Pirate whenever an artifact enters. Professional Face-Breaker makes a Treasure when your creatures connect and lets you sacrifice a Treasure to play the top of your library this turn. You spend that mana on another Pirate, a removal spell, or the attack that kills someone.
- **A normal turn:** Attack with the Pirates you have, crack the Treasures, and cast the next Pirate or a piece of interaction before blockers matter again. End step, Brass steals if three of them hit the same player.
- **Support categories:** Treasure makers, a Pirate to wear the counters and the Equipment, cost reduction, interaction, card draw off the Treasures
- **Exemplars:** Malcolm, Keen-Eyed Navigator, Captain Lannery Storm, Gemcutter Buccaneer, Captain Storm, Cosmium Raider, Corsair Captain, Herald's Horn, Professional Face-Breaker
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4. The mana is in the combat, so a developed board casts two spells a turn. Herald's Horn makes Pirate spells cost {1} less and can put a Pirate into your hand at upkeep. It is about $6. Professional Face-Breaker is about $6 and is not a Pirate.
- **Profile fit:** Combat, tribal, and a mana engine that serves the attack. The Treasures are spent on the crew and on interaction. This is not a sacrifice combo.

### C. Open the lane

- **How it wins:** Three Pirates connect on one player, and you take their best nonland permanent. You do it again next turn. Cover of Darkness names Pirate and gives them fear, so only artifact creatures and black creatures can block. Storm Fleet Sprinter has haste and cannot be blocked. Departed Deckhand cannot be blocked except by Spirits, and {3}{U} gives another creature the same restriction for the turn. Changeling Outcast is every creature type, including Pirate, and cannot be blocked. Breeches, Eager Pillager can make one creature unable to block. Warkite Marauder turns one defending creature into a 0/1 with no abilities. Kitesail Larcenist turns one artifact or creature each player controls into a Treasure for as long as the Larcenist stays on the battlefield. Fathom Fleet Captain can pay {2} on attack, if you control another nontoken Pirate, to make a 2/2 Pirate token with menace.
- **A normal turn:** Play one evasive Pirate, point three Pirates at the player who has the permanent you want, clear a blocker if fear is not enough, and take that permanent at end step. Attack with the stolen permanent next turn.
- **Support categories:** fear and unblockable, one-creature answers for blockers, menace tokens, interaction to protect the three attackers
- **Exemplars:** Cover of Darkness, Storm Fleet Sprinter, Departed Deckhand, Changeling Outcast, Breeches, Eager Pillager, Kitesail Larcenist, Fathom Fleet Captain
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3. The steal is the win, so the deck is built to turn that trigger on. A sweeper sets the count back to zero. Cover of Darkness is about $22.
- **Profile fit:** This is Brass's own line: combat, then take a permanent. The 99 exists to get three Pirates through. No stax and no extra turns.

## Stop

Locked: **B, with a C lane package.** Treasure crew, plus fear, unblockable, and one-creature answers so three Pirates can connect. The list is in `03-deck.md` and `deck.cod`. Pitiless Plunderer, Timestream Navigator, and Ragavan stayed out.
