# Stage 1 — Commanders / shells

- **Idea:** A Pirate deck. Combat tribal, treasures, and attack-trigger value.
- **Format:** commander
- **Bracket / role target:** high Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4
- **Colors / identity (if known):** open
- **Scryfall queries used:**
  - `t:pirate is:commander legal:commander` ordered by edhrec
  - `is:commander o:pirate -t:pirate legal:commander` ordered by edhrec
  - `card` checks: Admiral Beckett Brass; Malcolm, Keen-Eyed Navigator; Breeches, Brazen Plunderer; Edward Kenway; Breeches, Eager Pillager; Don Andres, the Renegade; Ramirez DePietro, Pillager; Captain Storm, Cosmium Raider; Mary Read and Anne Bonny; Francisco, Fowl Marauder; Admiral Brass, Unsinkable; Vaan, Street Thief; Captain Lannery Storm

Left off the shortlist: **Admiral Brass, Unsinkable** (mill four, then return a Pirate from the graveyard with a finality counter; that is a graveyard deck). **Don Andres, the Renegade** (stolen creatures become Pirates; the deck is theft, and the tribe is incidental). **Ragavan, Nimble Pilferer** (about $41, and the deck is dash-and-steal goodstuff). **Mary Read and Anne Bonny** (the treasure asks you to discard the Pirate). **Breeches, the Blastmaker** (coin-flip copy). **Dargo, the Shipwrecker** (sacrifice-cost combo partner, not a crew). **Captain Howler, Sea Scourge**, **Xavier Sal, Infested Captain**, **Kari Zev, Skyship Raider**, and **Captain N'ghathrod** (Pirate on the type line, different deck). Malcolm paired with Kediss is the fast treasure combo line; this shortlist keeps Malcolm with a Pirate partner.

None of the commanders below are Game Changers.

## Options

### 1. Malcolm, Keen-Eyed Navigator and Breeches, Brazen Plunderer

- **Identity / colors:** UR
- **Why it fits the pitch:** Both are Pirates with partner. When one or more Pirates you control deal damage to opponents, Malcolm creates a Treasure for each opponent dealt damage, and Breeches exiles the top card of each of those libraries. You may play those cards this turn and spend mana as though it were any color.
- **Likely plans:** Evasive Pirates hit more than one player in the same combat. Treasures pay for the stolen spells the same turn, and the stolen spells keep the pressure on. Blue and red hold the counters and the haste.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4. The engine is in the command zone and starts at three and four mana. Together they are under $1.
- **Risk:** Each trigger pays once per opponent, not once per Pirate, so a single connect on one player is one Treasure and one card, and Breeches's card expires at the end of the turn. No black.
- **Skip if:** you want the stolen cards to stay available past this turn, or you want black removal in the identity.

### 2. Edward Kenway

- **Identity / colors:** UBR
- **Why it fits the pitch:** At the beginning of your end step, create a Treasure for each tapped Assassin, Pirate, and/or Vehicle you control. When a Vehicle deals combat damage to a player, exile the top card of that library face down. You may play it for as long as it stays exiled.
- **Likely plans:** Crew Vehicles with Pirates, attack, then convert the tapped crew into Treasures. The Vehicle hits are the card advantage, and they do not expire at the end of the turn. Grixis has the removal and the counters.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4. Four mana, three colors, and the Treasure count scales with the board. About $9.
- **Risk:** Vehicles want creatures to crew them, and the Treasure count wants those same creatures tapped. The list can slide into Vehicles and stop being a Pirate deck.
- **Skip if:** you want the commander to pay off Pirates with no Vehicles in the plan.

### 3. Admiral Beckett Brass

- **Identity / colors:** UBR
- **Why it fits the pitch:** She is the pirate lord. Other Pirates you control get +1/+1. At the beginning of your end step, gain control of target nonland permanent controlled by a player who was dealt combat damage by three or more Pirates this turn. The steal does not wear off.
- **Likely plans:** Wide evasive Pirates. The anthem makes the attacks real. Three connects on one player take their best permanent. Treasures in the 99 pay for the crew.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3. Four mana. The anthem is reliable; the steal is the spike. About $0.30.
- **Risk:** Three Pirates have to connect against the same player. Blockers and a sweeper turn the steal off, and she does not draw cards or make Treasures herself.
- **Skip if:** you want the command zone to produce mana or cards even when the attack fails.

### 4. Ramirez DePietro, Pillager

- **Identity / colors:** UB
- **Why it fits the pitch:** When he enters, you lose 2 life and create two Treasure tokens. Whenever one or more Pirates you control deal combat damage to a player, exile the top card of that player's library. You may cast it for as long as it remains exiled.
- **Likely plans:** Dimir Pirates. The enter trigger is a small burst of mana. Connecting keeps a stolen spell on the table until you want it, backed by black removal and blue counters.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3. Three mana, two colors, clean mana. About $0.25.
- **Risk:** The life loss repeats every time you recast him. The trigger cares about combat damage, so pingers do not count. No red haste.
- **Skip if:** you want red in the pirate package, or you do not want a life payment on the commander.

### 5. Francisco, Fowl Marauder and Malcolm, Keen-Eyed Navigator

- **Identity / colors:** UB
- **Why it fits the pitch:** The black partner pair. Francisco explores whenever one or more Pirates you control deal damage to a player. Malcolm still makes a Treasure for each opponent dealt damage. Explore keeps the land drops honest while the Treasures pay for the next pirate.
- **Likely plans:** Evasive Pirates hit several players. Explore finds the next land or creature; Treasures cast it. Dimir interaction sits behind the attacks.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3. Francisco is two mana and Malcolm is three. Together they are under $1.
- **Risk:** Explore is a smaller payoff than Breeches's theft. Francisco cannot block. Two commanders means two tax tracks.
- **Skip if:** you would rather steal cards than filter your own library, or you want red.

### 6. Breeches, Eager Pillager

- **Identity / colors:** R
- **Why it fits the pitch:** First strike. Whenever a Pirate you control attacks, choose one mode you have not chosen this turn: create a Treasure, make a creature unable to block this turn, or exile the top card of your library and play it this turn. Three attacking Pirates take all three modes.
- **Likely plans:** Mono-red aggro. Attack, make a Treasure, clear a blocker, and impulse your own top card. The deck is your cards, not theirs.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3 aggro. Three mana, one color, the fastest crew here. About $0.50.
- **Risk:** Each mode works once per turn, so the fourth Pirate does not add another Treasure. Mono-red interaction is thin against a sweeper, and the impulse is your library, not an opponent's.
- **Skip if:** you want the attacks to steal from opponents, or you want a second color.

### 7. Captain Storm, Cosmium Raider

- **Identity / colors:** UR
- **Why it fits the pitch:** Whenever an artifact you control enters, put a +1/+1 counter on target Pirate you control. Treasures, Vehicles, and rocks are all triggers. The crew gets larger because the deck is making artifacts.
- **Likely plans:** Cheap Pirates plus a steady stream of Treasure. Stack the counters on an evasive Pirate and turn the mana into more artifacts. Izzet still has the counters and the wheels that are actually card draw.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3. Two mana, so she is out early, and the payoff lives entirely in the 99. About $0.30.
- **Risk:** She has no evasion and no attack trigger of her own. One counter per artifact goes on a single Pirate, so the deck can become one huge creature instead of a boarding party.
- **Skip if:** you want the commander to pay you for connecting, not for playing artifacts.
