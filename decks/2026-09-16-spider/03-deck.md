## Game plan

Ramp into Shelob. Cheap deathtouch spiders (`Deadly Recluse`, `Attercop`) and fight/bite (`Ulvenwald Tracker`, `Bite Down`, `Khalni Ambush`, `Ram Through`) kill their best creatures; Shelob turns those kills into Food copies. `Academy Manufactor` and `Peregrin Took` multiply the snacks; `Night of the Sweets' Revenge` turns Foods into green mana and an overrun. Defense is the other half: `Arachnogenesis` fogs a swing and dumps spiders, `Maze of Ith` plus `Gyome` (Food → indestructible) keep the board, `Toxic Deluge` resets when Shelob's toughness lets her live. `Ishkanah` is a second spider engine and a drain finish.
