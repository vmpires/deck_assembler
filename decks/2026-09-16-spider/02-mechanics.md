# Stage 2 — Mechanics

- **Pick:** Shelob, Child of Ungoliant
- **Format:** commander
- **Bracket / role target:** Bracket 3
- **Idea reminder:** Spiders, reach, toughness > power. Shelob is {4}{B}{G} — the 99 stays **light** (most creatures CMC ≤3) and **mana-heavy** so she is on time and recastable.

Shelob constraint: she is the only expensive spider. Do not stack 5–8 drops that compete with her. Recast plan is **reduce cost** (Urza's Incubator naming Spider → first Shelob is {2}{B}{G}) plus **cast from hand** (Command Beacon / Netherborn Altar skip commander tax) plus **dorks and 2-mana land ramp**. Gaea's Cradle is legal and a nuclear recast land (~$1600) — flag, do not require. Nykthos (~$53), Urborg (~$58), Cabal Coffers (~$32), Delighted Halfling (~$30) are the chase mana pieces.

Skipped: Thantis-style forced attacks, graveyard-first (Spider Spawning as a theme), Marvel Spider-Hero pile, Craterhoof-sized haymakers, Game Changers (Demonic Tutor, Survival of the Fittest, Cradle as a must-include).

## Shared mana kit (every plan)

Dorks (Llanowar Elves, Fyndhorn Elves, Elves of Deep Shadow, Birds of Paradise, Twitching Doll), 2-mana forests-untapped (Nature's Lore, Three Visits, Farseek), Cultivate / Kodama's Reach / Skyshroud Claim, Cryptolith Rite (every spider taps for mana), Urza's Incubator / Herald's Horn, Command Beacon, Netherborn Altar, Fanatic of Rhonas (four green once anything is 4 power; Shelob is 8).

## Plans

Propose 2–4 distinct game plans. Exemplar cards must already be confirmed with `scripts/scryfall.py`.

### A. Cheap lethal spiders

- **How it wins:** Flood cheap reach/deathtouch spiders, drop Shelob, swing. 1/2s become lethal. Combat, not a combo.
- **A normal turn:** Turn 1 dork, turn 2 Nature's Lore or a 2-mana spider, turn 3 Incubator or another land, turn 4 Shelob. After that, attack with Deadly Recluse / Attercop / Snarespinner under deathtouch.
- **Support categories:** CMC ≤3 spiders, shared ramp kit, a couple of anthems (Wall Crawl), point removal, recast lands.
- **Exemplars:** Deadly Recluse, Attercop, Mirkwood Spider, Snarespinner, Wall Crawl, Ruins Recluse.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** Bracket 3
- **Profile fit:** Tribal + combat. Lightest 99. Closest to “lots of spiders” without a high curve.

### B. Token web

- **How it wins:** Make many 1/2 reach spiders, then Shelob makes the swarm lethal. Overrun is a mop-up, not the deck.
- **A normal turn:** Ramp, play Arasta or Twin-Silk Spider, sit. Opponents’ instants make more spiders. Shelob lands; Cryptolith Rite turns the web into mana to recast her.
- **Support categories:** Token makers, shared ramp kit, Cryptolith Rite, one fog-style spider dump (Arachnogenesis), few 4-drops only.
- **Exemplars:** Arasta of the Endless Web, Twin-Silk Spider, Arachnogenesis, Twitching Doll, Curse of Clinging Webs, Wall Crawl.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** Bracket 3
- **Profile fit:** Tokens + tribal. Heavier on 4-drops than A. Skip Ishkanah / Lolth to keep the curve honest.

### C. Fight the web

- **How it wins:** Spiders fight or bite, the other creature dies, Shelob makes a Food copy of it. You steal their best bodies as snacks and win the attrition war, then attack.
- **A normal turn:** Ramp into Shelob. Bushwhack or Ram Through a spider into their threat. Eat the Food copy. Repeat with Ulvenwald Tracker.
- **Support categories:** Cheap fight/bite, shared ramp kit, cheap deathtouch spiders so fights are one-sided, a little protection so Shelob survives the fight.
- **Exemplars:** Bushwhack, Ram Through, Bite Down, Ulvenwald Tracker, Khalni Ambush, Hunter's Talent.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** Bracket 3
- **Profile fit:** Combat + interaction. Shelob’s unique trigger is the point. Fewer spider names in the 99; more spells. Bushwhack also ramps if you do not need the fight.

## Stop

Waiting for a pick. Do not write the full list yet.

**Recommendation:** **A** (cheap spiders + the shared mana kit). That is the light recast deck. Take **B** if you still want Arasta’s factory in the 99 as a second engine. Take **C** if you care more about Shelob copying what she kills than about spider density.
