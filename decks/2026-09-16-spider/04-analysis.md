- **Source:** `list.txt` in this folder (Plan A: cheap lethal spiders).
- **Format:** commander
- **Current bracket on file:** Bracket 3, Game Changers listed as none — **wrong**. `Sol Ring` and `Deadly Rollick` are Game Changers. Real count: **2**.
- **Target:** high Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4 (competitive). Keep ≤3 Game Changers unless you ask for full B4.

## Why it feels like a regular creature deck

Plan A did what it said: 22 cheap reach spiders, anthems (`Wall Crawl`, `Metallic Mimic`, `Realmwalker`, `Patchwork Banner`, `Blex`), ramp into Shelob, attack. Without Shelob, most of those bodies are 1/3–1/5 reachers that do not win races or lock the board.

Shelob’s unique line is unused. She copies a creature as Food when a spider deals combat damage to it and it dies. The 99 only has `Ram Through` and `Bushwhack` to force that. There is **no Food payoff**. So the commander is a deathtouch lord, not a theft engine.

## Holes vs a defensive, spicy Shelob

1. **No spider fog.** `Arachnogenesis` was in Plan B and never made the list. That is the defensive signature card.
2. **No board wipe.** Deathtouch does not save you from a faster go-wide deck if Shelob is dead.
3. **Food copies have nowhere to go.** Night of the Sweets' Revenge (Foods tap for G + overrun), Academy Manufactor, Peregrin Took, Gyome (Food → indestructible, also a defensive tap).
4. **Fight package is two cards.** Ulvenwald Tracker, Bite Down, Khalni Ambush, Hunter's Talent make Shelob steal commanders and haymakers.
5. **No tutor.** For high B3, `Worldly Tutor` (creature, not a Game Changer) and/or `Demonic Tutor` (third GC) belong here.

## Plans — pick one

Exemplars confirmed on Scryfall. Commander stays Shelob.

### D. Defensive web (recommended)

- **How it wins:** Sit behind reach + `Arachnogenesis` + a wipe, Shelob makes blockers lethal, then steal what attacked you as Food and beat down.
- **A normal turn:** Ramp, deploy Arasta / Sporeweb Weaver. Opponent swings; fog into a spider army. Shelob + Gyome makes the Food copies and the team hard to kill.
- **Support:** Arachnogenesis, Gyome, Tamiyo's Safekeeping, Maze of Ith, Toxic Deluge or Pernicious Deed, Ishkanah or Lolth.
- **Bracket:** high B3 (keep Sol Ring + Deadly Rollick; add no third GC yet).
- **Risk:** Still not a combo deck. Needs Shelob or Arachnogenesis to feel like more than walls.

### E. Shelob's pantry (the spice)

- **How it wins:** Fight/bite their best creature with a deathtouch spider → Food copy → Manufactor / Night of the Sweets' Revenge / Peregrin. You play *their* threats as snacks and overrun off Food count.
- **A normal turn:** Shelob in play. Tracker or Bite Down a spider into their commander. Copy it. Repeat.
- **Support:** Ulvenwald Tracker, Bite Down, Khalni Ambush, Hunter's Talent, Academy Manufactor, Night of the Sweets' Revenge, Peregrin Took. Cut the vanilla 1/4s to pay for this.
- **Bracket:** high B3.
- **Risk:** Fight needs Shelob or native deathtouch or you trade down. Foods without Night/Manufactor/Gyome are just 3-life snacks.

### F. Competitive Golgari (lean B4)

- **How it wins:** Same combat plan, but you find Shelob / Arachnogenesis / wipe on time and the draw is real.
- **Support:** Worldly Tutor, Demonic Tutor (3rd GC), Sylvan Library (swap a GC if you want this instead of Tutor), Damnation or Toxic Deluge. Cut Cultivate, tapped duals, Fatal Push.
- **Bracket:** top of B3 with 3 GCs, or B4 if you add a fourth (`The One Ring` or `Sylvan Library` on top of Tutor + Sol Ring + Rollick).
- **Risk:** Looks less “spider tribal,” more Golgari goodstuff with a spider commander unless D or E is in the same pass.

## Pick

**D + E** (2026-09-17). No F tutors. Game Changers stay at 2: `Sol Ring`, `Deadly Rollick`. List rebuilt in `list.txt` / `deck.cod`.
