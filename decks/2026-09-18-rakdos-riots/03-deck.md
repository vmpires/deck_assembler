## Game plan

Ping an opponent **this turn** with cheap burn (**Tectonic Hazard**, **Chain Lightning**, **Boltwave**, **Flame Rift**, **Price of Progress**) or a fast attacker (**Ragavan**, **Vexing Devil**, **Stormfist Crusader**, **Broadside Bombardiers**). Cast **Rakdos, Lord of Riots** when you have a giant, or just attack.

**Primary dump:** **Blightsteel Colossus** / **Ulamog** / **Kozilek** cheap or free after life loss. **Electrodominance** dumps a fatty without Rakdos. **Ilharg, the Raze-Boar** attacks a creature from hand (Eldrazi can still connect uncast).

**Combat backup (if the 10-drops miss):** **Broadside Bombardiers**, **Neheb, the Eternal**, **Inferno Titan**, **Scourge of the Throne** (extra combat vs the life leader), **Balefire Dragon**, **Bloodletter of Aclazotz** (doubles life loss on your turn — Rakdos 6/6 flying is a real clock). **Anger**, **Fervor**, **Rising of the Day**, **Greaves**, **Boots**, **Arena of Glory**, and **Hanweir Battlements** give *your* team haste.

**Chandra's Ignition** on Blightsteel is 11 poison to each opponent. **Purphoros**, **Terror of the Peaks**, and **Impact Tremors** still tax enters.

Midrange pass: cut 10–13 CMC bricks and Eldrazi-only support; keep all seven Game Changers and the fast mana.
