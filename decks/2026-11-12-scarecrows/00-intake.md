# Stage 0 — Intake

- **Idea:** Scarecrow tribal / typal. Does a scarecrow deck make sense, and which commanders can carry it.
- **Format:** commander
- **Bracket / power target:** high Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4 (default). At most 3 Game Changers. No MLD, no extra-turn chains, no stax.
- **Colors / identity:** open. True type-matters wants five colors (Reaper King). Artifact-creature and -1/-1 plans can sit in fewer colors.
- **Refusal / keep:** No stax, prison, chaos, or cEDH. Graveyard and politics only if the pick is about them (persist / steal, or Rendmaw goad).

## Reality check (Scryfall)

A scarecrow deck **does** make sense, but not as a dense lord-tribal like Elves or Goblins.

- **52** Commander-legal Scarecrows. Most are colorless artifact creatures; many are small utility bodies (mana, wither, persist, color-matters keywords from Lorwyn / Shadowmoor).
- Cards that actually say "Scarecrow" in rules text: **Reaper King** (lord + ETB destroy a permanent), **Scarecrone** (draw / reanimate artifact creatures), **Wickersmith's Tools** (the only scarecrow token maker), plus **Boggart Arsonists** (anti-scarecrow, ignore).
- Four legendary Scarecrows exist. Only Reaper King rewards the creature type. The other three are Scarecrows on the type line and do something else (goad birds, -1/-1 theft, insect delirium).
- Glue that makes the tribe playable at this power level: changelings (`Universal Automaton`, `Changeling Outcast`, `Mirror Entity`, `Realmwalker`), type-changers (`Maskwood Nexus`, `Arcane Adaptation`, `Rukarumel, Biologist`), blink, and the fact that almost every scarecrow is an artifact creature.

**What works:** Reaper King as a removal engine; overlaying "everything is a Scarecrow" onto a real creature deck; artifact-creature combat that happens to be scarecrows; wither / -1/-1 using the Shadowmoor package.

**What does not:** stuffing 30 gray 2/2s into a 99 and hoping a +1/+1 lord carries Bracket 3.
