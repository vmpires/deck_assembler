# Stage 1 — Commanders / shells

- **Idea:** Scarecrow tribal / typal — playable only if the commander rewards the type, overlays the type, or uses a mechanic the tribe already has (artifact creatures, wither).
- **Format:** commander
- **Bracket / role target:** high Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4
- **Colors / identity (if known):** open
- **Scryfall queries used:**
  - `is:commander o:scarecrow`
  - `t:scarecrow is:commander`
  - `t:legendary t:scarecrow`
  - `t:scarecrow legal:commander`
  - `o:scarecrow legal:commander`
  - `is:commander o:"choose a creature type" legal:commander`
  - `is:commander o:"artifact creature" legal:commander`
  - `keyword:changeling t:creature legal:commander`
  - `is:commander o:"-1/-1 counter" legal:commander`
  - named lookups: Reaper King; The Reaper, King No More; Rendmaw, Creaking Nest; The Swarmweaver; Wickersmith's Tools; Morophon, the Boundless; Rukarumel, Biologist; Urza, Chief Artificer; Alibou, Ancient Witness; Graaz, Unstoppable Juggernaut; Maskwood Nexus; Arcane Adaptation

## Options

Rank 5–8. For Pauper, these are archetype shells, not commanders.

### 1. Reaper King

- **Identity / colors:** WUBRG (five-color)
- **Why it fits the pitch:** The only commander whose text cares about Scarecrows. Other Scarecrows get +1/+1, and each one that enters destroys a permanent. Hybrid cost casts for WUBRG (five mana) or up to 10 generic. Confirmed legal in Commander (`usd` ~$12).
- **Likely plans:** Scarecrows + changelings + `Maskwood Nexus` / `Arcane Adaptation` so every body is a removal spell; blink / bounce to retrigger; artifact ramp and Scarecrone loops. Combat is backup after the board is stripped.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** high Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4. Keep Game Changers at 3 (`Cyclonic Rift`, a tutor, `Rhystic Study` or similar — named later if picked).
- **Risk:** Five-color manabase. The printed scarecrow pool is thin; without type-change and changelings the destroy trigger is too sparse. `Painter's Servant` is a scarecrow and a chase card (~$49) — include only if you want it.
- **Skip if:** you do not want five colors.

### 2. Rukarumel, Biologist

- **Identity / colors:** WUBRG (five-color)
- **Why it fits the pitch:** Names Scarecrow on ETB. Every nontoken creature you control (and your sliver tokens) is a Scarecrow. `Reaper King` in the 99 then destroys a permanent for almost every creature that enters, not just the ~20 scarecrows worth playing. Makes a 1/1 Sliver, which is also a Scarecrow.
- **Likely plans:** Midrange creatures that are already good, painted as scarecrows; Reaper King as the hidden commander; changelings and a few real scarecrows for texture; blink Rukarumel only if you need to rename (you will not).
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** high Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4. This is the most competitive "scarecrow" shape because the 99 does not have to be bad tribal filler.
- **Risk:** Tokens from the rest of the deck are **not** Scarecrows unless they are Slivers. Flavor is "everything is a scarecrow," not a skulkin tribal pile. Five-color still.
- **Skip if:** you want the 99 to look like scarecrows, not like a 5-color creature deck wearing the type.

### 3. The Reaper, King No More

- **Identity / colors:** BRG (Jund)
- **Why it fits the pitch:** A legendary Scarecrow that uses the other half of the tribe: wither, persist, and -1/-1 counters (`Lockjaw Snapper`, `Grim Poppet`, `Dread Tiller`, `Wickersmith's Tools`, the color-matters scarecrows). ETB spreads -1/-1 counters; once per turn you steal an opponent's creature that dies with those counters.
- **Likely plans:** Wither combat + counter distribution, then theft as the win. Scarecrone and persist recycle bodies. Tighter manabase than five-color. No Reaper King (needs white and blue).
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** high Bracket 3. Theft is slow (once a turn) unless the -1/-1 engine is dense.
- **Risk:** Stealing dead creatures is adjacent to graveyard play (the card never hits the graveyard if you take it, but persist / reanimate packages will show up). Not a type-matters lord.
- **Skip if:** you want "scarecrow ETB destroys the world" rather than wither / theft.

### 4. Morophon, the Boundless

- **Identity / colors:** WUBRG (five-color)
- **Why it fits the pitch:** Changeling, names Scarecrow, gives the team +1/+1, and reduces **colored** costs of Scarecrow spells by WUBRG. `Reaper King` in the 99 can cost zero mana. Morophon itself is a Scarecrow once named.
- **Likely plans:** Reaper King as a free-ish payoff; changelings (they have colored pips, so they actually get cheaper); a core of printed scarecrows that do not get cheaper (most are generic).
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** high Bracket 3. The lord is real; the cost reduction is not, for the bulk of the tribe.
- **Risk:** Seven mana before reduction. Colorless skulkins ignore the discount. Weaker Reaper King delivery than just casting Reaper King as commander, unless you specifically want Morophon as the 5-mana-off enabler.
- **Skip if:** you already like Reaper King in the command zone more — you probably should.

### 5. Urza, Chief Artificer

- **Identity / colors:** WUB (Esper)
- **Why it fits the pitch:** Scarecrows are almost all artifact creatures. Urza gives them menace, affinity for artifact creatures, and a Construct token that scales with artifacts. Competitive artifact-creature plan; scarecrows are the body of the tribe, not a type payoff.
- **Likely plans:** Artifact-creature combat with value tokens; Scarecrone as recursion; no Reaper King (missing red and green). Premium Esper interaction keeps it at the target bracket.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** high Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4.
- **Risk:** This is an artifact-creature deck with scarecrow art, not scarecrow tribal. `Urza, Lord High Artificer` is a different card and a Game Changer — do not confuse them; Chief Artificer is the one listed here.
- **Skip if:** the type line "Scarecrow" must matter in the command zone.

### 6. Alibou, Ancient Witness

- **Identity / colors:** RW (Boros)
- **Why it fits the pitch:** Combat-first artifact creatures, which matches the profile. Haste on the team; each attack turns tapped artifacts into damage and scry. Cheap colorless scarecrows are on-curve beaters.
- **Likely plans:** Ramp into Alibou, swing with an artifact army, burn the table as a second clock. No Reaper King, no blue card selection.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** Bracket 3 combat. Can reach high 3 with a dense mana-rock and interaction package; less naturally a 4 than Urza or Reaper King.
- **Risk:** Two colors means worse scarecrow payoffs and worse interaction. Boros artifact decks can flood into rocks and miss a win.
- **Skip if:** you want control / ETB destruction more than attacking.

### 7. Rendmaw, Creaking Nest

- **Identity / colors:** BG (Golgari)
- **Why it fits the pitch:** Legendary Scarecrow. Plays cards with two or more types (artifact creatures are already two types, so scarecrows count) and goads 2/2 Birds for the table.
- **Likely plans:** Multi-type value (artifact creatures, enchantment creatures, artifact lands) + goad tokens. Politics / pillow-adjacent combat redirection.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** Bracket 3 goad / tokens. Not a scarecrow-type payoff.
- **Risk:** Profile treats politics as not-default. Birds are not Scarecrows. The deck is "play gold-type cards, goad the table."
- **Skip if:** you do not want goad / group politics. **Default skip unless you ask for it.**

### 8. Graaz, Unstoppable Juggernaut

- **Identity / colors:** colorless
- **Why it fits the pitch:** Every scarecrow you control becomes a 5/3 Juggernaut. You can play the entire colorless scarecrow pile, including Scarecrone and Painter's Servant, with no color identity fights.
- **Likely plans:** Artifact ramp into a hay-bale trampling army. Colorless interaction only.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** low-to-mid Bracket 3 unless we add colorless combo. Likely undershoots the default high-3 / low-4 target.
- **Risk:** Cannot run Reaper King, The Reaper, Rendmaw, or any colored scarecrow. Colorless interaction is clunky. Eight-mana commander.
- **Skip if:** you want the default power band, or you want Reaper King in the deck.

## Not recommended

- **The Swarmweaver** — legendary Scarecrow, but the text is Insect / Spider delirium. That is a different deck.
- **The First Sliver** — changelings are Scarecrows, and Reaper King fits, but the deck becomes cascade Slivers (~$36 commander, Bracket 4 shape). Not a scarecrow pitch.
- **Yawgmoth, Thran Physician** / **Hapatra, Vizier of Poisons** — better -1/-1 commanders than The Reaper, but they abandon the scarecrow identity (and Yawgmoth is aristocrats / graveyard, which is not a default).
