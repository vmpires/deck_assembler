- **Name:** Elemental Death Burn
- **Format:** commander

- **Mechanic:** Extra land drops make 5/5 Elementals; they burn on the way in, and a wipe or sacrifice turns the army into a table kill

## Game plan

Play extra lands (`Exploration`, `Burgeoning`, `Azusa, Lost but Seeking`, `Oracle of Mul Daya`, `Manabond`) so Omnath makes 5/5 Elementals. `Warstorm Surge` and `Where Ancients Tread` burn on the way in. The close is a death turn: `Blasphemous Act` kills the 5/5s, and each death is 3 from Omnath to any target. `Chandra's Ignition` on Omnath (or on `Multani, Yavimaya's Avatar` / `Ulvenwald Hydra` when they are larger) hits each opponent and kills the army. `Fiery Emancipation` triples that damage and survives the wipe. `Insult // Injury` doubles the turn you fire and makes the damage unpreventable.

`Fling` and `Soulblast` throw a body at a player and still get the death triggers. `Goblin Bombardment` and `High Market` sacrifice one Elemental at a time. `Deflecting Swat` is free while Omnath is out and retargets a counter aimed at the kill spell.

Do not give the army indestructible on the turn you want it to die. `Glacial Chasm` and `Constant Mists` stay; Chasm stops damage dealt to you, not the burn you deal.
