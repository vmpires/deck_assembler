- **Format:** commander

- **Mechanic:** Balanced. Green ramp, white defense, red haste and removal, power-5 creatures with enter and attack triggers. Creatures stuck in hand go back into the library for a new grip.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4 (2 Game Changers)

## Game plan

Mayael costs `{R}{G}{W}`. Her ability costs `{3}{R}{G}{W}` and looks at the top five cards for a creature with power 5 or greater. Ramp to that six, protect her, and activate. Twenty-six of the ninety-nine are those creatures. Enter triggers draw, fight, destroy, make tokens, or deal damage. Attack triggers do the same once haste is online. Craterhoof Behemoth closes a wide board.

Elvish Piper and Quicksilver Amulet put a creature from your hand onto the battlefield. Scroll Rack and Valakut Awakening do the other job: the creature goes back into the library and you draw something else, so a hand full of haymakers does not stall the game.

## Creatures in hand

**Scroll Rack** (`{2}`, then `{1}`, `{T}`). Exile any number of cards from your hand face down. Draw that many cards from the top of your library. Then put the exiled cards on top of your library in any order.

Use it when the hand is creatures you cannot cast yet.

1. Exile those creatures.
2. Draw that many new cards. Lands, ramp, and interaction come to hand.
3. Put the creatures on top in the order you want.
4. Activate Mayael. She looks at the top five, which now starts with those creatures, and can put one onto the battlefield.

The same Rack can do this again on a later turn.

**Valakut Awakening** (`{2}{R}`, instant). Put any number of cards from your hand on the bottom of your library, then draw that many cards plus one. The back face, Valakut Stoneforge, is a tapped Mountain if you need the land instead.

This one draws an extra card. The creatures go to the bottom, so Mayael will not see them until the library turns over. Use it when you need a new hand more than you need those creatures on top. Scroll Rack is the one that sets up her activation.

Gwenna adds mana that can cast a creature spell or activate a creature's ability, so she can pay for Mayael. The +1/+1 counter happens when you cast a power-5 creature, not when Mayael puts one in. Somberwald Sage adds three mana only for casting creature spells. She does not pay for the activation.

Natural Order only finds a green creature. Craterhoof, Ghalta, Titan of Industry, Kogla, Thorn Mammoth, Giant Adephage, Quartzwood Crasher, Xenagos, Etali, Primal Conqueror, Zacama, Archetype of Endurance, and Vorinclex are the hits worth tutoring.

Zacama untaps lands only if you cast it. Mayael and Natural Order skip that. The 9/9 and the damage and destroy abilities still work.

Kozilek draws four when you cast it, not when Mayael puts it onto the battlefield. The attack makes the defending player sacrifice four permanents. If Kozilek goes to the graveyard, that graveyard is shuffled into the library.

Vorinclex doubles your land mana and stops each land an opponent taps from untapping on their next untap. That is a stax effect. It is in the list because it was in the Cockatrice file.

Dragonlord Dromoka stops opponents from casting spells during your turn. Avacyn gives your other permanents indestructible. Sigarda has hexproof and stops opponents from making you sacrifice permanents.

Return of the Wildspeaker and Where Ancients Tread came out to make room for Scroll Rack and Valakut Awakening. Rishkar's Expertise still draws off power. Terror of the Peaks still deals damage when a creature enters.
