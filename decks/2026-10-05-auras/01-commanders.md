# Stage 1 — Commanders / shells

- **Idea:** Aura decks. Suit creatures with Auras and win through that engine.
- **Format:** commander
- **Bracket / role target:** high Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4
- **Colors / identity (if known):** open
- **Scryfall queries used:**
  - `is:commander (o:aura OR o:"enchanted creature" OR o:"aura spell")` ordered by edhrec
  - `card` checks: Light-Paws, Emperor's Voice; Pearl-Ear, Imperial Advisor; Uril, the Miststalker; Mazzy, Truesword Paladin; Three Dog, Galaxy News DJ; Ellivere of the Wild Court; Ivy, Gleeful Spellthief; Eriette of the Charmed Apple; Wyleth, Soul of Steel; Galea, Kindler of Hope; Calix, Guided by Fate; Yenna, Redtooth Regent; Killian, Ink Duelist

Left off the shortlist: **Killian, Ink Duelist** (already built; Auras that target a creature cost `{2}` less). **Wyleth, Soul of Steel** and **Galea, Kindler of Hope** (Aura or Equipment, so the deck slides into equipment). **Bruna, Light of Alabaster** (graveyard is the engine). **Yenna, Redtooth Regent** and **Calix, Guided by Fate** (copy engines that drift into infinite loops or generic enchantress).

## Options

### 1. Light-Paws, Emperor's Voice

- **Identity / colors:** W
- **Why it fits the pitch:** Every Aura you cast may tutor another Aura with equal or lower mana value and a different name, then attach that card to Light-Paws. One spell becomes two permanents, and the second one does not have to be cast.
- **Likely plans:** Voltron. Cast a cheap Aura, fetch protection or a bigger Aura straight onto the fox, and attack. The tutored Aura was not cast, so it does not chain a second search.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4. The most consistent list here. Mono-white mana is simple; the fox is `{1}{W}`.
- **Risk:** The game is the same every time: find one Aura, tutor the package, swing. Wraths and sacrifice effects ignore the Auras. Commander tax hurts if she dies before the pile sticks.
- **Skip if:** you want games that do not play out as a fixed tutor line.

### 2. Uril, the Miststalker

- **Identity / colors:** RGW
- **Why it fits the pitch:** Hexproof, and he gets +2/+2 for each Aura attached to him. Opponents cannot target the suited creature. Naya has the deepest Aura pool (white and green).
- **Likely plans:** Bogles. Ramp to four mana, stick Uril, stack Ethereal-Armor-style pump and totem armor, and one-shot people. The 99 has to draw the Auras; he does not tutor or draw.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3. Four mana and three colors make him slower than Light-Paws, and hexproof makes the kill hard to interact with once he is suited.
- **Risk:** Hexproof does nothing against board wipes or edicts. If Uril is not on the battlefield, the deck is a pile of Auras with nothing to wear them.
- **Skip if:** you want card draw or tutoring in the command zone.

### 3. Pearl-Ear, Imperial Advisor

- **Identity / colors:** W
- **Why it fits the pitch:** Enchantment spells cost `{1}` less for each Aura you control. Casting an Aura that targets a modified permanent you control draws a card. Lifelink on the body.
- **Likely plans:** Cast a lot of Auras. The first Aura modifies a creature; every Aura after that on that creature draws. Costs fall as the pile grows, then you attack with the discounted stack.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3. Card advantage and a cost reducer, without Light-Paws's locked tutor line.
- **Risk:** The first Aura on an unmodified creature does not draw. No hexproof. You can draw into more Auras than you have mana or bodies for if the reducer is offline.
- **Skip if:** you want the tutor consistency of Light-Paws in the same color.

### 4. Mazzy, Truesword Paladin

- **Identity / colors:** RGW
- **Why it fits the pitch:** Any enchanted creature that attacks an opponent gets +2/+0 and trample until end of turn. When an Aura you control dies, exile it; you may cast it until the end of your next turn.
- **Likely plans:** Spread Auras across several attackers so each one tramples. Removal of an Aura gives a recast window. Naya pump Auras, not a single voltron target.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3. Wider than Uril, so one removal spell does not end the turn.
- **Risk:** The +2/+0 and trample last only for the attack. The recast is a short window, not a graveyard engine. Still needs real pump Auras to kill.
- **Skip if:** you want one giant creature rather than a team.

### 5. Three Dog, Galaxy News DJ

- **Identity / colors:** RW
- **Why it fits the pitch:** When you attack, you may pay `{2}` and sacrifice an Aura attached to Three Dog. Each other attacking creature gets a token copy of that Aura attached to it.
- **Likely plans:** Make a few attackers, wear one strong Aura on Three Dog, then pay 2 on the attack step and copy it across the team. One Aura spell becomes a board of copies.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3. Explosive when you have bodies; quiet when you do not.
- **Risk:** You sacrifice the original. You need other attackers, and Boros is short on ramp. Token Auras vanish when the creature dies.
- **Skip if:** you want the Aura to stay on one protected creature.

### 6. Ellivere of the Wild Court

- **Identity / colors:** GW
- **Why it fits the pitch:** When she enters or attacks, she makes a Virtuous Role on another creature you control. That Role gives +1/+1 for each enchantment you control. Whenever an enchanted creature you control deals combat damage to a player, you draw a card.
- **Likely plans:** Enchantress combat. Stack enchantments so the Role is large, attack with several enchanted creatures, and draw for each one that connects. Selesnya ramp supports the enchantment count.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3. Value and a growing board, less all-in than voltron.
- **Risk:** A new Role from her replaces an older Role you control on that creature. She does not stack eight Auras on herself. The draw only happens on combat damage, so blockers stop the engine.
- **Skip if:** you want classic bogles (one hexproof creature, many Auras).

### 7. Ivy, Gleeful Spellthief

- **Identity / colors:** GU
- **Why it fits the pitch:** Flying. Whenever a spell targets only a single creature other than Ivy, you may copy it onto Ivy. A copy of an Aura spell becomes a token Aura.
- **Likely plans:** Keep a second creature, cast an Aura at that creature, and Ivy gets a copy. Blue adds cheap interaction. Two suited fliers instead of one.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3. Simic interaction plus a copy engine. The Aura tokens are not cards, so they do not restock your hand.
- **Risk:** You need a second creature or the copy never happens. You may decline the copy, including when an opponent aims removal at your other creature. Token Auras do not come back.
- **Skip if:** you want the Auras to be real cards you can replay.

### 8. Eriette of the Charmed Apple

- **Identity / colors:** BW
- **Why it fits the pitch:** A creature enchanted by an Aura you control cannot attack you. At your end step, each opponent loses life and you gain life equal to the number of Auras you control.
- **Likely plans:** Auras on their creatures to shut off attacks, Auras on yours still counting toward the drain. The win is life loss, not commander damage.
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3, on the slower side. Orzhov, same colors as the existing Killian list, different job.
- **Risk:** A board full of "can't attack" Auras plays like a soft lock. Burn, unenchanted creatures, and enchantment removal still kill you. This is the pillow option.
- **Skip if:** you want to attack, or you want nothing that keeps people from swinging.
