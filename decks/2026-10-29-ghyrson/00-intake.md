# Stage 0 — Intake

- **Idea:** An Izzet deck whose cards deal exactly 1 damage, so Ghyrson Starn turns each of those hits into 3. Named from the fun-mechanics shortlist.
- **Format:** commander
- **Bracket / power target:** high Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4. At most 3 Game Changers in the 99. Competitive speed, a real win, interaction. Never cEDH. Full Bracket 4 only if asked.
- **Colors / identity:** Izzet (**U/R**) — **Ghyrson Starn, Kelermorph**
- **Refusal / keep:** No stax, hard locks, mass land destruction, extra-turn chains, or chaos. Graveyard and politics stay off the plan. Two-card infinites only if asked later. Sources that deal exactly 1 damage to you stay out, because Ghyrson would deal 2 more to you.
