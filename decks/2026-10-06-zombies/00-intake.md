# Stage 0 — Intake

- **Idea:** Zombie tribal. A typal deck that wants Zombie creatures, lords, and a commander that pays off a Zombie board.
- **Format:** commander
- **Bracket / power target:** high Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4
- **Colors / identity:** UB (Wilhelt, the Rotcleaver)
- **Refusal / keep:** No stax, prison, chaos, or extra-turn chains. Graveyard recursion only as support for the tribe, not a reanimator pile. No infinite loops unless asked.
