# Stage 2 — Mechanics

- **Pick:** Wilhelt, the Rotcleaver
- **Format:** commander
- **Bracket / role target:** high Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4
- **Idea reminder:** Dimir Zombie tribal. Wilhelt is a Zombie. Another Zombie you control dying makes a 2/2 decayed Zombie token, unless that Zombie already had decayed. At your end step you may sacrifice a Zombie and draw a card. Decayed tokens cannot block, and they are sacrificed at the end of combat if they attacked.

Left out of every plan: Rooftop Storm plus Gravecrawler loops, Phyrexian Altar, and any sacrifice outlet used to go infinite. Undead Warchief is a Zombie lord and a cost reducer (`{2}{B}{B}`, Zombie spells cost `{1}` less, Zombies get +2/+1) and is about $18.

## Plans

### A. Lords and a combat clock

- **How it wins:** Zombie lords turn a board of small Zombies into real attackers. Death Baron gives other Zombies +1/+1 and deathtouch. Lord of the Accursed gives other Zombies +1/+1 and can grant menace. Diregraf Captain gives other Zombies +1/+1. Hordewing Skaab gives other Zombies flying, then loots when Zombies deal combat damage to opponents. Lost Monarch of Ifnir gives other Zombies afflict 3, so a blocked Zombie still costs the defender 3 life, and a Zombie connecting lets you mill three and return a creature card to your hand. Decayed tokens attack once under those buffs and then die.
- **A normal turn:** Play a lord, attack with the Zombies you already have, and let Wilhelt draw if you can spare a body at end step. The decayed token from a trade joins the next attack.
- **Support categories:** anthems, evasion, deathtouch, a few cheap Zombies to wear the buffs, interaction
- **Exemplars:** Death Baron, Lord of the Accursed, Diregraf Captain, Hordewing Skaab, Bladestitched Skaab, Lost Monarch of Ifnir
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4. The clock is combat. Wilhelt's draw keeps the lords coming.
- **Profile fit:** Combat, tribal, and attack-trigger value. Hordewing Skaab is about $6. The decayed tokens are attackers for one combat, so the durable Zombies have to be the lords and the nontoken creatures.

### B. Deaths drain the table

- **How it wins:** A nontoken Zombie dies, Wilhelt replaces it with a decayed token, and the death also drains. Diregraf Captain drains an opponent for 1. Plague Belcher and Vengeful Dead drain each opponent for 1. Undead Augur draws a card and you lose 1. Wilhelt's end step is another sacrifice and another card. Ghoulcaller Gisa sacrifices a creature and makes 2/2 Zombie tokens equal to its power. Those tokens do not have decayed, so a later death turns each of them into a Wilhelt token too. Carrion Feeder, itself a Zombie, sacrifices a creature for a +1/+1 counter. Viscera Seer sacrifices a creature to scry 1.
- **A normal turn:** Cast a Zombie, sacrifice an older nontoken Zombie to Feeder or Seer, take the decayed token and the drain, and draw at end step. Attack with whatever is still large enough.
- **Support categories:** sacrifice outlets, death drains, death draw, token replacement, a few lords so the leftovers can attack
- **Exemplars:** Diregraf Captain, Plague Belcher, Vengeful Dead, Undead Augur, Headless Rider, Ghoulcaller Gisa, Carrion Feeder, Viscera Seer
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3 / low Bracket 4. The drain starts as soon as Zombies die, before the board is big enough to kill someone in combat.
- **Profile fit:** This is Wilhelt's own text: deaths, tokens, and a card. Headless Rider makes a 2/2 Zombie token when a nontoken Zombie dies, and that token does not have decayed. The plan stays a tribe with drains. It does not add a two-card infinite.

### C. Build the horde, then cash it in

- **How it wins:** Make Zombies that do not have decayed, then let deaths convert them. Diregraf Colossus enters with a +1/+1 counter for each Zombie card in your graveyard, and creates a tapped 2/2 Zombie whenever you cast a Zombie spell. Cryptbreaker discards a card to make a 2/2 Zombie, and three tapped Zombies draw a card. Endless Ranks of the Dead creates X 2/2 Zombies at your upkeep, where X is half the Zombies you control, rounded down. Necroduality copies each nontoken Zombie that enters. Champion of the Perished gets a +1/+1 counter whenever another Zombie enters. Jadar, Ghoulcaller of Nephalia makes one decayed Zombie at your end step if you control none. The win is a huge board attacking, with Wilhelt replacing the ones that die.
- **A normal turn:** Cast a Zombie spell, take the Colossus token, copy the nontoken with Necroduality, put a counter on Champion, and attack. End step, sacrifice one body to Wilhelt and draw.
- **Support categories:** Zombie token makers, copy effects, a sacrifice draw, lords from Plan A as finishers
- **Exemplars:** Diregraf Colossus, Cryptbreaker, Endless Ranks of the Dead, Necroduality, Champion of the Perished, Jadar, Ghoulcaller of Nephalia
- **Bracket (EDH) or role (Pauper):** High Bracket 3. Endless Ranks and Necroduality are four mana. The board gets large, then one wrath sets it back to whatever you can recast. Necroduality is about $9. Diregraf Colossus is about $5.
- **Profile fit:** Tokens and tribal. The graveyard only matters as the counter count on Diregraf Colossus and as cards Cryptbreaker discarded. This is the slowest of the three to start attacking for lethal.

## Stop

Locked: **A and C together.** Lords and a combat clock, plus the token horde. A short drain package (The Meathook Massacre, Diregraf Captain, Plague Belcher, Gray Merchant of Asphodel) is the second win. Ramp and interaction are both heavy. The list is in `03-deck.md` and `deck.cod`. Rooftop Storm, Phyrexian Altar, and Pitiless Plunderer stayed out.
